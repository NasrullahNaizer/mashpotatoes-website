Our Philosophy

Why MashPotatoes?

We focus on what actually matters for small businesses: speed, clarity, and results.

Built for small businesses

We don't believe a small business needs a complicated website.

No endless technical meetings or unnecessary bloatware. We give your business clean pages, high-converting contact buttons, and everything your customers need to buy from you.

Designed, not templated

Every project is customized around the business.

We never paste your content into a generic cookie-cutter template. Your brand colors, photography, services, and neighborhood character shape every layout choice we make.

Mobile first

Your customers are on their phones. Your website should be ready.

Over 80% of local traffic comes from smartphones. We craft touch-friendly buttons, instant loading speeds, and one-tap WhatsApp integration so mobile visitors take immediate action.

Affordable

Professional doesn't have to mean expensive.

Big agency bills belong to big tech conglomerates. We offer honest, fair pricing tailored to local independent businesses, cafes, shops, and tradespeople.
